Why we built this

My name is Rich Diaz. For 25 years, I built brands, led sales teams and sat in the executive chair, and my family got what was left. Nights and holidays belonged to someone else.

Every company ran the same play. Pay thousands for a new lead. Call. Text. Watch most walk away. The next batch lands, and the old names go to sleep.

More ads. More leads. A bigger bill. Every month the number got harder to hit, and every month it cost another night at home.

Then we found the next sale in the one place we walked past every single day. Our own CRM.

We opened it, and there they were. Thousands of people who had once leaned in and said “tell me more.” Bought with our money and our time. Every one frozen mid-sentence.

Their interest never died. The conversation did.

These leads weren't lost. They were asleep. And a sleeping lead needs one thing: a wake-up call.

We built it. A system that finds the sleeping leads in your database and brings them back to life.
